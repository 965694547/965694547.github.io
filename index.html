About Me

I am now working at Qwen Team of Alibaba Group, leading Omni Pre-training in the Omni & Audio Group. If you are interested in academic cooperation, please feel free to email me at A6769408@hotmail.com. I am hiring interns!

I received my M.S. degree from the Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences in 2024, and my B.S. degree from Jilin University in 2021.

I am the founder and core contributor of the natively end-to-end omni-modal foundation model series Qwen-Omni (Qwen2.5-Omni, Qwen3-Omni, Qwen3.5-Omni, Qwen3.8-Omni). The series' open-source releases each crossed 2,000+ GitHub stars on the day of release and ranked #1 on the HuggingFace Trending leaderboard, with 8,100+ GitHub stars and 1,000+ Google Scholar citations accumulated to date.

My research interest includes audio understanding, omni understanding and omni-modal foundation models. I have published 20+ papers with 35,000+ citations at top-tier international AI conferences such as ICLR, ACL, AAAI and ACM MM.
